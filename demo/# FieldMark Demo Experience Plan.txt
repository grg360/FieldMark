## Methodology Overview Page

### Audience
- MSL who has never seen the platform
- Reads in 2 minutes max
- Should leave the page MORE trusting, not less

### Sections to cover
- How Rising Star scoring works (60/40 Scientific/Network, Pharma informational)
- How Established cohort differs (same 60/40, different framework for "who matters today")
- What the three signals actually measure
- What we DO use (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, OpenAlex, NPPES, CMS Open Payments)
- What we DO NOT use (commercial data, prescribing data, proprietary sources)
- How Scientific Positions extraction works (senior/first-authored only)
- What confidence numbers mean (and don't mean)
- Limitations honestly stated

### Principles
- Show our work
- Acknowledge tradeoffs
- No marketing voice — analyst voice
- Plain language, no jargon-without-definition

## First-Login Wizard

### Goals for first 60 seconds
- User understands FieldMark is a research intelligence platform, not a CRM
- User sees one HCP profile that demonstrates the depth
- User finishes with one watchlist and one tracked HCP

### Screen sequence (draft)
1. Welcome + role confirmation (MSL? Medical Director? Other?)
2. Territory setup (states + therapeutic area)
3. Suggested HCPs to start tracking (3-5 from your territory)
4. Show one HCP profile with annotation overlays explaining sections
5. Land on /me with the first watchlist created

### Open questions
- How do we handle users who skip the wizard?
- What if their territory has no Rising Stars?
- Do we re-trigger any of this on their second visit?

## Demo Experience (live demo with mentors)

### Flow
- Start at /me (show personalized workspace)
- Click into one tracked HCP (Heymach for thoracic MSL, swap by mentor)
- Show Scientific Narrative section
- Click a theme → drawer with paper-grouped evidence
- Click DOI → land on actual paper
- Back to profile, show cohort score breakdown
- Generate brief on the fly
- Cross-reference to coverage gaps in territory

### What I want each mentor to walk away saying
- "I haven't seen this anywhere else"
- "I could trust this in a regulated environment"
- "The provenance chain is the whole game"
