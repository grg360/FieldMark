with a FieldMark profile. No environment exists. The recommended first
implementation would isolate source, database, network, datasets, secrets and
evidence on Umbra K11, then prove resettable application mechanics with

## What changed

Flatten and identity blockers are retired under reported controls. Dedup merge
is removed from Breast replay. OpenAlex remains shared and is bounded through
input admission. The development agent reports bounded TA generalization with

## Ontologist role

The Ontologist may later propose Breast trial vocabulary, subtype/biomarker
mappings and Scientific Pulse themes/signatures with synonyms, exclusions and
collision warnings. Every term must receive corpus hit counts, zero/high-result
and collision checks, representative publications, coherence review and
founder validation. No proposal is accepted under this ticket.

## What the Lab proves—and does not

It can prove migration/configuration mechanics, Lab RLS, bounded linkages,
scoring/UI, vocabulary behavior, reset/rerun and NSCLC regression under Lab
conditions. It cannot prove production availability, scale, live-role
security, cross-TA shared-state safety, production idempotency or activation
readiness.

## Next authorization

The proposed Lab Foundation boundary covers only isolated platform/profile
plumbing, empty Lab resources, schema/migration bootstrap, health, evidence,
reset/destruction and synthetic Tier 1 fixtures. It excludes public corpus
queries, vocabulary execution/acceptance, Breast ingestion, provider use,
production credentials, dedup merge and promotion.
