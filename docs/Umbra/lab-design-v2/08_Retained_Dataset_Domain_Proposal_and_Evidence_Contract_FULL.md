## Package invariants

All datasets, proposals, configurations and evidence are immutable,
content-addressed, scoped to an application profile and authorization, and
checked before use. A hash change creates a new package; it never mutates an
accepted record. Raw credentials, unrestricted production rows and
unnecessary personal identifiers are excluded.

## Dataset contracts

Tier 1 uses synthetic structural fixtures only. Tier 2 contains bounded,
dated, approved public-source extracts and inclusion/exclusion rules. Tier 3
contains reviewed sanitized regression cases. Manifests record source,
licensing/authority, privacy class, indication, counts, schema, hashes,
creation/expiry and intended assertions.

## Domain proposal contract

The Ontologist may propose Breast trial condition/intervention terms, subtype
and biomarker mappings, Scientific Pulse themes/signatures, synonyms,
exclusions, ambiguity and concept-ontology-lag warnings. Equivalent NSCLC
structures are patterns, never Breast content. New agents and mechanisms may
require title/abstract signatures when ontology concepts lag.

The Ontologist does not query PubMed, OpenAlex, ClinicalTrials.gov, NPPES,
FieldMark, Supabase or a Lab corpus under design authority. Proposal creation
and corpus validation are separately authorized roles.

## Empirical validation contract

Every term/signature later records:
- intended concept and signature type;
- corpus hit count;
- zero-result and implausibly-high-result checks;
- acronym/short-term collision checks;
- representative matched publications;
- exclusions and collision risk;
- theme-coherence review where applicable;
- Ontologist confidence and revision history;
- validation and founder acceptance states.

Corpus mechanics produce counts and samples. The Ontologist interprets
failures and revises proposals. The founder reviews the evidence and accepts or
rejects vocabulary. Plausibility alone never advances beyond
AWAITING_CORPUS_VALIDATION.

## Calibration and decision register

Breast Community volume floor and P95 patient/payment/drug scaling, Established
thresholds and KOL board/surname calibration are corpus-derived. KOL relevance
requires founder or qualified domain-expert judgment. The Rising model
(legacy v3 versus AD-style composite) is DEFERRED_FOUNDER_DECISION. No numeric
values are pre-populated.

## Evidence contract

Each LabRun produces source/schema/migration/config/dataset/proposal hashes;
authorization and network facts; stage status; tests; row and relationship
counts; term hits and representative samples; identity and duplicate guards;
resource/spend; screenshots where useful; failures; truth labels and
limitations. Claims distinguish repository expectation, static schema
structure, LAB_OBSERVED_FUNCTIONAL_BEHAVIOR, production-observed evidence,
inference and unresolved assumption.

Evidence is retained when the Lab resets or is destroyed. Lab observations
must never be relabelled as production proof.
