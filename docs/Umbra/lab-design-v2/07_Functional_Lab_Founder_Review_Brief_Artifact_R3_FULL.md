## Executive takeaway

The design is now intentionally simpler for the first Breast proof. It uses a
managed FieldMark worktree and isolated local PostgreSQL on K11. The database
is bootstrapped from repository-root `schema.sql` plus the live-object manifest
in `sql/README.md`; repository migrations are not treated as a complete
clean-room baseline.

## What changed

- Containerization moved to `FOLLOW_ON_PLATFORM_MILESTONE` after a known-good Breast
  functional build exists.
- Egress is `DEFAULT_DENY_WITH_RUN_SCOPED_ALLOWLIST`.
- Approximately 210 scripts are discovery scope; only explicit
  `LAB_COMMAND_ADMISSION_MANIFEST` entries may run.
- Commands with target-version behavior must specify `--target-version v2`.
- Exit zero is not success; silent zero output is `FAILED_VALIDATION`.

## What remains accepted

The Lab remains functional proof, not production-safety proof. Dataset and
DomainProposalPackage governance, empirical vocabulary validation, durable
evidence, dedup-merge exclusion, OpenAlex input bounding, founder calibration,
KOL review and the deferred Rising-model decision are unchanged.

## Recommended posture

`READY FOR FINAL DEVELOPMENT REVIEW`

The next possible founder decision is a bounded `LAB_FOUNDATION_AUTHORIZED`
record covering the managed worktree, isolated local PostgreSQL, local
processes and Tier 1 fixtures. It would not authorize a Breast corpus run,
production Supabase, FieldMark production mutation, container parity, or
production promotion.

## Provisional resource range

- Foundation engineering: approximately 3–6 focused engineering days after
  final command and bootstrap confirmation.
- Host class: 8–16 vCPU and 24–48 GB RAM, validated before authorization.
- Storage: 20–40 GB steady and 50–80 GB temporary cap; reported flatten
  evidence establishes an 802 MB relation and roughly 1.6 GB transient
  two-copy floor, not a guaranteed Lab measurement.
- Foundation reset/bootstrap target: 10–30 minutes after commands are known.
- Provider cost: `$0` unless separately authorized.
