v3 · what changed, and what is flagged EXCLUSION REPLACES CORRECTION
1
EXCLUSION, NOT CORRECTION
The divisor is gone. Only posts at least fourteen days old count, and their totals are compared raw.
Dividing by days up assumed response arrives at a steady rate. It does not — it front-loads and saturates — so the divisor flattered recent posts instead of protecting them, and the old panel said the opposite of what it did. Excluding the unsettled fortnight needs no model of how response accrues: it only needs the posts to have stopped gathering it. Verified against the corpus 31 July: capture has run twice — 20 May to 3 June, then again from 21 July — so the included posts run 20 May to 3 June (18,526 of 19,379), and all 853 posts from the resumed run sit inside the fortnight and are held for the next pass. The eligibility boundary is 17 July; no post exists between 4 June and 20 July for it to admit.
2
THE CORPUS CHIP IS GATED
Rendered only where a person confirmed the match — verified_by_human = true, the same gate as the DOL panel. It links nowhere.
A match row alone would surface 88 medium-confidence matches and the 11 held after review, misattributions among them. The header states the gate rather than leaving a reader to assume the chip means the same as a match, and rows without it read "unmatched" — which is the truth about our knowledge, not a claim about the person.
3
THE LABEL NOW MATCHES THE METRIC
The order is stated as response received. Presence is not claimed in the label — it enters the total, and the post count sits beside every figure so you can see it doing so.
With raw totals, volume contributes: Kettle Ridge's 104 posts carry it to fourth on 4,420 responses, and its row says plainly that this is forty-two responses a post against Nadia Oyelaran's four hundred. The reverse case is stated too — @osimertinib_dad has the highest response per post on the page and sits sixth, because we chose totals. Anyone who wants the other ordering can see what it would do.
THE THREE VERIFY-BEFORE-EXPORT ITEMS · CUT, CONFIRMED, STATED
Cut · "conversations joined · started by others". Resolving who started a thread needs the conversation root, and roots may sit outside the corpus. The rows now show original versus reply, which needs only is_reply — the split is gone rather than guessed.
Confirmed · the atopic dermatitis figures. 41 posts, 12 accounts and the 14 July start were illustrative — the corpus was checked on 31 July and immunology capture has not started: 0 accounts, 0 posts, no capture date. The panel now renders the real zeros, and the empty state stands on its own terms.
Stated · the minimum to show a list. The audit set no threshold, so the surface no longer implies one: fifty accounts, named as our choice. If someone defends a different number, this is the one line to change.
