PHONE STRATEGIC JUN 6, 2026 EDIT

On a steering committee call following the LAURA trial readout, Heymach pushed back hard on the framing that osimertinib's benefit in stage III EGFR-mutant NSCLC validates a chemotherapy-free consolidation paradigm broadly. His point: the biomarker context is everything — EGFR-mutant patients are precisely the population where layering IO on top adds toxicity without a clear immunologic rationale. He's not against consolidation strategies, but he's drawing a sharp line between oncogene-driven and IO-eligible populations.
