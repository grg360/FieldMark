Notes
Hierarchy

Tagline first (78px serif, left field), amber Sign in second, form third, LinkedIn fourth. The FM mark drops to a 24px corner signature — it no longer competes.

The space is the design

Full-bleed sky with a 500px sign-in column pinned to the right edge on desktop, and a bottom sheet on mobile. Nothing floats over a void; the negative space is sky, and the sky is left of the eye path.

How the split collapses at 390

The two columns rotate from side-by-side to stacked: sky on top, sign-in as a bottom sheet with a 22px top radius reading as a horizon. The sheet is sized to its content, not the viewport — 498px resting, 596px with the error banner — so the sky shrinks rather than the form scrolling. Tagline drops 78 → 42px and keeps its two-line break; the eyebrow survives, the sub-copy does not. Every target stays ≥ 44px (fields 48, buttons 50, Install app 44), and the compliance line moves inside the sheet so nothing sits on bare sky.

LinkedIn, in register

Ghost button on the platform's ivory-on-near-black; recognition carried by the in tile in a desaturated slate blue (#2A4E70). Amber stays the only saturated colour.

Cheap atmosphere

Sky is CSS only — one gradient, two layered radial-gradient starfields, seven SVG circles and lines. No images, no canvas, no JS. The form is markup at first paint; the 90s drift and 11s twinkle are decorative layers that can be dropped for reduced-motion.

Legibility

Fields never sit directly on sky — the column and sheet carry a 72–95% near-black scrim plus blur. Focus is an amber border with a 3px amber halo; error is a warm terracotta (#CB5C44) reserved for failure and used nowhere else.

Not rendered

The constellation is seven anonymous points in an invented shape. No names, institutions, counts or real co-authorship structure appear on this public route.
