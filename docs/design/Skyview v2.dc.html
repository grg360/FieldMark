The exploration model
One sky, a free camera
Stars never move. Focus is only where the camera is looking, so nothing is ever modal — drag to pan, scroll to close in, click to settle.
Roam
The nearest star to the cursor brightens, magnifies and names itself, and its co-authorship lines warm up. Discovery without committing to anything.
Hop
Every orbit star is a destination. Click one — in the sky or in the panel — and the camera glides there and blooms their orbit. Walk the network star to star; the route stays as a breadcrumb you can fly back along.
Full-bleed, measured not assumed
The sky fills whatever box it is given — a ResizeObserver drives the camera, viewBox, chrome insets and panel width off the measured element, so there is no fixed pixel size anywhere. Use the Viewport tweak to preview 1280 → 3440. Because a camera frames by both axes, a wider screen shows more sky rather than a stretched one.
Off-field stars are destinations too
Heymach still reveals Zhang 97 · Gibbons 91 · Wistuba 89 · Swisher 73 · Le 62 — three outside the recognized field. Those three are clickable: the camera travels, and the panel says plainly why there's no ranking behind them.
