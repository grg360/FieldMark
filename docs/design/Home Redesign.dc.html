The landing decision

Home leads with what moved, not what is overdue. One intelligence moment, stated as a sentence, above the queue.

A to-do list is a surface any CRM renders, and it opens the session on the MSL's guilt. Only FieldMark can say Piro Lito climbed 35 places in your territory since 8 June, and here is the record that placed him there. That sentence is the product, so it reads first.

The queue is not demoted, it is re-motivated: it sits directly beneath the movement band, and each action carries a why now line drawn from the same record. Overdue stays visible from the first pixel as a standing figure in the masthead.

Hierarchy
01Masthead ledger — standing figures: overdue, open, tracked, coverage 02What moved — one banded movement with its trace, then two rows 03Next 3 actions — each with a why-now line 04Follow-ups by urgency 05The record — insights, briefs, relationship activity RRail: territory, institutions, portfolio, invitations
Lens patch

Neutral hairlines, mono metric stacks with two-line captions, steel institution lines, trace blocks with open-through links, dashed cards where the record is withheld, band rules over grouped rows. Gold is load-bearing only: rank, movement, primary action.
