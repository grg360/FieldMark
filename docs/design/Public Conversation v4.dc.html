v4 · the chart, and what it is not allowed to claim GEOMETRY KEPT · CLAIMS CUT
1
WHAT IT IS CALLED, AND WHEN
"Rising voices — last 90 days" becomes Audience against response · every account in the window, over 20 May – 3 June 2026.
The title now names the two axes and nothing else — no rising, no arrival, no claim about when anyone started. The window correction is not chart-only: capture paused on 3 June and resumed on 21 July, and the resumed run sits entirely inside the fourteen-day exclusion, so the included range is fifteen days and the list below moved with it. Post counts, response totals, the group boundary at 2,000 and the order itself are all re-derived on those fifteen days.
2
COLOUR CARRIES ONE STORED FACT
The three classes are gone. Gold means one thing: a corpus match a person confirmed — the same gate as the row chip.
"Rising voice / established with engagement / other voices" was a classification of people wearing the clothes of a legend, and this surface does not classify people. Nine of the 128 plotted accounts carry a confirmed match; the rest read "not matched, or matched without confirmation", which is a statement about our records rather than about them. The legend says so in as many words.
3
THE DENOMINATOR IS DISCLOSED WHERE IT IS USED
The ratio stays; the disclosure sits between the title and the plot, not in a footnote.
Dividing by an undated follower count is a stronger claim than printing one, so the panel says the axis rests on a single unrecorded reading and asks the reader to treat vertical position as a band. The x-axis label repeats it. The floor is stated the same way: three or more included posts, a number we chose — re-derived down from ten, because ten posts in fifteen days is a different population from ten in ninety.
4
HOVER, PIN, AND THE ACCOUNT THAT IS NOT IN THE LIST
Hover reads out in a fixed slot under the plot; click pins it there. The list never jumps.
Almost every dot is an account the list does not show — 3,104 accounts, seven rows — so pinning cannot mean "scroll to the row". The pinned card renders the same fields a row would, says where the account sits in the order, and states plainly that it is not in the list below. Its representative post follows the same withholding rules, and if the account is one of the seven, the pinned card marks the row instead of duplicating it.
NOT BROUGHT ACROSS
Hot topics · stays out. Nothing labels a post with a topic; the hashtag strip does the honest version of the job and says what it is.
Share of voice · recommend dropping it. A donut of post-volume share measures our capture, not the conversation — "Others 78%" was mostly a statement about what we crawled. The scatter already shows the whole population, and per-account volume is in the list. If it returns, it needs a denominator someone can defend.
Trending vs prior week · not built. With a fifteen-day included range and a seven-week gap in capture, there is no comparable prior week inside the window.
Still flagged · the atopic dermatitis counts remain PENDING CONFIRMATION, the fifty-account list minimum and the three-post plot floor are both named as our choices, and the "started by others" split stays cut until conversation roots can be resolved.
