Placement · answering the eight-item bar
It goes on the wordmark side.
Not a ninth item, not a replacement. The eight are corpora — nouns you browse when you have a question. The Week is not a corpus; it is your own portfolio sliced by time, and it has an answer that changes weekly. Those are different kinds of object and they do not belong in the same row. The wordmark group already carries the one non-corpus destination in the product — HOME — and it is the only underused region of a bar measured as full. The Week joins it there, as a dated marker that states the week's fact.
WHY NOT A NINTH ITEM
Beyond the 124px overflow: a ninth label would be the only entry in the row whose contents change every Monday, and the only one scoped to you rather than to the corpus. Even with room it would read as misfiled.
WHY NOT A REPLACEMENT
SOCIAL is the tempting cut — 140 verified accounts against thousands. But The Week draws on it as a source, and removing a reference surface to make room for a digest of that surface is a trade the MSL loses. Nothing in the eight is substituted by The Week.
TWO ENTRY POINTS, NOT ONE
The marker and Home's line are complementary. Home's line catches the user who lands there anyway; the marker is what makes a Tuesday with no question a reason to click. Card 04 stands — it just needed the second half.

1A
FieldMark · new surface
The Week
What happened to the thirty-three people this MSL tracks, over the seven days just closed. Its own surface, not a section of Home. Ordered by facts we already hold, never by a score. Most weeks it is nearly empty, and that state is designed first.
01 — SPARSE IS THE NORMAL STATE
The quiet week is the first frame, not the fallback. It carries one sentence of fact, the coverage ledger, and the last thing that did happen — dated. No blank panel, no zero, no apology. A user who opens it on a Tuesday and finds nothing has still learned something: that nothing was missed.
02 — WHAT COUNTS AS AN EVENT
No score. Three facts already held, printed in the open: position in the author list, whether the work touches a theme already on their record, whether it sits in your therapeutic area. The ordering rule is printed above the list so it can be argued with. Fortieth author on a consortium paper is not hidden — it falls to ALSO ON THE RECORD, present but not promoted.
03 — SINCE WHEN
The unit is the calendar week. Always true, identical for every user, renders with no session state. last_dashboard_view_at is used for one thing only — a rule drawn across the list marking where you stopped. Return after a month and the current week is whole; the four behind it collapse to one line each, opened on demand.
04 — WHERE IT LIVES
Its own surface. Home answers what should I do; The Week answers what happened. Nothing on Home is replaced — WHAT MOVED, THE RECORD, next actions and follow-ups all stay. Home gains one line: the week's count and its first event, linking here. The reason to navigate is that the answer is small and personal, and Home is neither.
05 — EVENTS AND ACTIONS
An event never becomes a task. It offers one control — attach to a follow-up, existing or new — with the event as its citation. Where a follow-up for that HCP is already open, the event says so and offers nothing further. The default is no action, and a week where nothing is attached is a complete week.
