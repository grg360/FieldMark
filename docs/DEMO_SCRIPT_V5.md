2. **Warm Heymach's brief.** Generate it, confirm five opportunities
   render, then leave it alone — the 24h cache means it'll serve
   instantly on camera.
3. **Pick Beat 11's paper.** Open Heymach's bibliography, find a
   **senior-authored, threadless** paper that connects to one of his
   belief claims. Write its title and PMID into Beat 11 below.
4. **Rehearse the Beat 11 question** against the content check. It
   rejects recommendation phrasing, off-label vocabulary and physician
   names. Get a pass before recording.
5. **Check WHAT MOVED on Home.** If it says "no qualifying movement,"
   skip that half of Beat 3.
6. **Confirm Social's top row** is Choueiri / Lopes / Liu / Pal, not
   the embassy account.
7. **Confirm SkyView** renders the full field, not 24 nodes.
8. **Walk the whole path once.**
